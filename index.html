FAQ

Why are tickets $50? Why isn’t this reunion free?

People are flying in from all over the country, and even all over the world, for this event. We want it to be worth your while: beautiful views, catered food, custom cocktails and a convenient location for those flying in.

Why are we meeting at a bar in the daytime and not the evening?

Because it costs $3,000 more to reserve the space after 4 p.m.

Do we have to leave the venue at 4 p.m.?

No. At that point it turns into a regular bar, open to the public. You’re welcome to keep eating and drinking, but our tab will be closed.

Can I stay overnight at the hotel?

Yes. The organizers are working to get a discounted room block at the hotel.

Can I bring my significant other, friends or family members to the main event?

No. They can stay at the hotel with you, but they’re not invited to the rooftop event. First of all, that’s our deal with the venue rental. Second, we want to drink and hang with you. Third, there’s an optional family-friendly event the next day.

Is this event organized by or affiliated with West Georgia or Alumni Services?

No. This event is independently organized by Kristen Turner and Laura Maughan. However, we’re in communication with Alumni Services to help spread the word.
